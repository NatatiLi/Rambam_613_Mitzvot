THE 613 MITZVOT — ORDER OF THE RAMBAM'S LIST, Hyamson's numbering (Positive 1–248, then Negative 1–365)
Verse text: JPS 1917 English (public domain), in the wording of the 1917 text ("ye" and "builded" kept). 

SOURCES (all public domain)
  • Numbering and wording of each mitzvah: the Rambam's list of the 248 positive and 365 negative commandments, in the order and numbering of Moses Hyamson's translation (printed lists are not numbered identically at every point; see the Notes) (Mishneh Torah, 1937–49; text from the Sefaria public-domain set).
  • Cross-checked against: Simon Glazer, Mishnah Torah (Yad ha-Hazakah) lists, 1927; and the uploaded scan "Book of the Precepts, Affirmative and Prohibitive" (Hebrew & English; Robert Young, Edinburgh; undated, after 1849), read from the page images where the OCR was garbled.
  nb: Young's volume is a Christian-published edition. Only the Rambam's list of the precepts and the Scripture citations in it were used; none of that other material.

POLICY
  1. One entry per mitzvah — 248 positive + 365 negative = 613. A verse that underwrites several mitzvot appears once for each (for example Devarim 26:14 carries Neg. 150, 151 and 152), because each is a different commandment.
  2. The primary verse is the one Hyamson attaches to the quoted proof-text of that mitzvah. Other verses cited for the same mitzvah are shown as "Also cited", with the source that cites them.
  3. Where a verse is already shown for an earlier mitzvah, its text is not repeated; the entry says so.
  4. A range of more than two verses shows only its first verse; the entry says which verses continue.
  5. Where Hyamson writes "ibid." the verse is the same as the preceding mitzvah's, confirmed against Young's citation.
  6. Where the sources disagree, the disagreement is stated on a "Note" line rather than settled by guess.

Pos. 71 — That an offering shall be brought by one who has in error committed a trespass against sacred things, or robbed, or lain carnally with a bond-maid betrothed to a man, or denied what was deposited with him and swore falsely to support his denial

    Also cited: Vayikra 5:21 (Young); Vayikra 19:20 (Young)
    Note: Hyamson gives no verse; the verses are taken from Young (Lev. 5:15-16; 5:21 [Eng. 6:2];
    19:20).
